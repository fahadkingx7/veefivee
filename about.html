HELLO, I'M THE MAKER BEHIND VEEFivee

I never really stopped playing with technology.

I've been coding for 15+ years, but the story started even earlier — with a childhood obsession for computers, games, and that irresistible question: “Wait… how does this work?”

From editing and rebuilding Grand Theft Auto games, creating mods and multiplayer gaming experiences, and sharing technical fixes on Microsoft community forums, I discovered that technology is most exciting when you use it to make something of your own.

That curiosity became a career of designing and developing custom websites, web applications, desktop software, automation systems and everyday computer tools. These days, I bring that same experimental spirit to AI, product design, marketing and solving real business problems.

That's why I built veefivee: a creative technology studio where smart engineering meets imagination, and no idea is too weird to explore.
