What exactly is TeamsLook?

TeamsLook is an AI call-intelligence product experience for sales and customer support teams. It brings together summaries, conversation outcomes, daily briefs, agent scorecards, follow-ups and management insights.

Can I explore TeamsLook without signing up?

Yes. The public demonstration is available at demo.veefivee.com/teamslook/. Use the demo username and password shown there (demo / demo). Its calls, people and metrics are simulated.

Does TeamsLook work for sales and support?

The demo includes dedicated Sales and Support perspectives. Sales focuses on leads, objections, conversion and callbacks; Support focuses on issue types, resolution, sentiment and escalations.

Can managers see which agents need coaching?

The product demo includes agent quality scores, skill areas, performance trends, comparisons and coaching recommendations. These are illustrative, based on simulated data.

Can it help us track callbacks and unresolved issues?

Yes, that is a core part of the demonstrated experience. Sales has callback priorities; Support includes open issues and escalations.

Can we ask questions about our call data?

The demonstration includes an Ask AI experience for questions about team activity and call patterns. Results in the demo come from simulated records.

Does it create downloadable reports?

Yes. The demo includes downloadable reports that present activity, trends, coaching information and team performance.

What integrations and call sources are supported?

Production integrations and setup requirements have not been publicly confirmed. Tell us which phone or CRM systems you use and we'll discuss the available approach rather than promising a connection we haven't verified.

How much does TeamsLook cost?

There is no confirmed public price list yet. Please contact the team with your expected team size and requirements.

Is the demo using real customer information?

No. The linked interactive demo explicitly uses fictional, simulated data. For live deployments, privacy, recording permissions and data-processing requirements should be discussed during onboarding.
