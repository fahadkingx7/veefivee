LET'S GET STARTED

You're just 3 steps away.

No technical setup required
01

Open the Web Store

Click the Chrome Web Store button above to visit the LogIn extension listing.

02

Add LogIn to Chrome

On the store page, choose Add to Chrome, then confirm Add extension if Chrome asks.

03

Open LogIn & sign in

Click Chrome's Extensions icon 🧩, open or pin LogIn, and sign in using the account details from your administrator.

✦

All set! Once you're signed in, LogIn will guide you to the sites your administrator has assigned. Need help with access? Contact your administrator.
