Use same glass header, GA4 tracking, canonical tags, sitemap. Keep soft gradient, use CSS only and respect reduced motion. Legal pages inherit LogIn policy styling with css/legal-pages.css small overrides. Avoid heavy images, scripts and unverified SEO claims.
