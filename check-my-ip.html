What does your IP reveal?

Your public IP identifies your internet connection to websites. It may suggest a city, region or network operator, but it does not reveal your exact street address. Location estimates can be inaccurate, especially with VPNs, proxies, mobile carriers and shared networks. The checker uses a third-party lookup service; opening this page sends a request to that provider. We don't ask for device location permission.
