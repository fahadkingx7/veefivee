Questions, answered.

How can I download my quote as a PDF?

Click Print / Save PDF and choose Save as PDF from the browser's printer destination. On iPhone, use the share or print options to save the document.

Can I keep client quotes for later?

Yes. Use Save draft to store it in the current browser, or Backup JSON to download a portable file. Drafts don't sync between devices.

Can I add taxes and discounts?

Yes. Enter a percentage discount and tax. The document shows subtotal, discount, tax, and grand total.

Does this replace accounting software?

No. This is a convenient document creator, not tax, legal or accounting advice. Confirm local invoicing requirements and rates for your business.
