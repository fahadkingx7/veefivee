Frequently asked questions

How is simple late payment interest calculated?

A common simple-interest illustration is principal times annual rate times overdue days divided by 365.

Can I charge any late fee I want?

No. Your contract and jurisdiction may restrict late fees and interest. Check before adding charges.

Does this calculate compounded interest?

No. It assumes simple interest with a 365-day year.
