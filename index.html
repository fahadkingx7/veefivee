THE PERSON BEHIND THE PIXELS

Curiosity has always been my superpower.

Hey, I'm the person behind veefivee — a developer, builder and lifelong tech explorer with 15+ years of hands-on coding under my belt.

My journey started long before “tech entrepreneur” sounded cool. As a kid, I was taking GTA games apart and putting them back together, creating mods and multiplayer experiences, and helping people solve problems on Microsoft forums. Somewhere between breaking things, fixing them and making them better, I fell in love with building.

Fast-forward to today: from custom web apps, software and clever computer tools to automation, AI and business growth, the same curiosity still drives every project. We build serious things — without losing the fun.
