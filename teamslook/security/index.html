Trust & transparency

Your conversations deserve careful handling.

A clear distinction between the simulated demo and a potential live deployment—and the important questions to ask before connecting real customer calls.

A demo with simulated data

The public TeamsLook demo uses generated calls and fictional people. It is designed to demonstrate the interface, not to expose actual customer recordings or personal data.

Discuss your real-world requirements

Before any production use, ask about data sources, access controls, data retention, call-recording consent, processing locations and integration boundaries. These should be agreed for your setup.

Responsible evaluation

Agent scoring and AI-generated recommendations should support human judgment, not replace review. Assess accuracy, fairness and context before acting on quality scores.

No unverified security claims

We haven't published formal claims of specific certifications or production security architecture on this site. Contact the team for details relevant to your intended deployment.
