Field guide / Follow-through

A promise isn't complete until it's kept.

A simple playbook for making customer follow-ups visible, owned and easier to complete.

The best conversation can still end badly if the next step vanishes. Build a consistent follow-up habit across sales and support.

1. Capture the commitment clearly.

What was promised, who owns it, when is it due and what information is required? “Call later” is not a complete next step.

2. Prioritize by urgency and impact.

Separate time-sensitive callbacks and frustrated customers from routine future work. Make overdue commitments easy to see, not easy to forget.

3. Keep the original context attached.

A follow-up becomes far more useful when it includes why the customer requested it and what happened previously.

4. Close the loop explicitly.

Confirm that the action happened and document the outcome. A contact attempt isn't always the same as resolution.

5. Learn from what keeps slipping.

If callbacks repeatedly go overdue, investigate the workflow: ownership, timing, scheduling, staffing or unclear commitments.
