Three steps. You're in.

No complicated setup needed.

01

Find & pin LogIn

Click the Extensions puzzle icon (🧩) in Chrome's toolbar. Find LogIn and pin it for easy access.

02

Sign in

Click the LogIn extension icon and enter the username and password provided by your administrator.

03

You're connected

Finish any device verification if prompted, then use LogIn to open the websites assigned to your account.

Don't have your sign-in details? Your administrator will provide your LogIn username, password, and assigned access. Please contact them if you need help signing in.
